## 📌 Problem Statement
With the rapid growth of **online education**, especially during and after COVID-19, ensuring fairness in online examinations has become critical.  
Fraudulent activities such as **identity theft** and **cheating** are prevalent, making it necessary to develop robust, AI-driven fraud detection systems.  

---

## 🎯 Objectives
✔️ **AI Fraud Detection** – Real-time detection using ML models  
✔️ **Identity Verification** – Facial recognition + keystroke dynamics  
✔️ **Privacy & Security** – Data protection & compliance  
✔️ **Cheating Prevention** – Suspicious behavior flagging  
✔️ **Scalability** – Support for mass online exams  
✔️ **User Experience** – Simple & seamless for students & admins

## 🔍 Fraud Detection Mechanisms
- 🎥 **Zoom SDK Recording** – Captures live video feed  
- 🧠 **Google Video Intelligence API** – Detects objects, faces, logos, text  
- 📄 **Plagiarism Detection** – Identifies repeated persons/objects & anomalies  

---

## 🧬 Biometric Authentication
- 👤 **Facial Recognition** – CNN-based (VGG-Face)  
- ⌨️ **Keystroke Dynamics** – ML trained typing patterns  
- 👀 **Eye & Head Tracking** – LSTM classification of movements
